Privacy Policy — Merge Depot

Last updated 19 October 2026

Merge Depot is published by Bathala. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and who it goes to. It covers the Android app only.

The short version: the game has no accounts, no sign-up, and never asks you for your name, email, or anything else about you. The data described below is collected automatically by the advertising and leaderboard services the game uses.

What is collected

Advertising identifier. Android gives each device a resettable advertising ID. Our advertising partner reads it to decide which ads to show and to count whether an ad was seen.

Device and network information. Device model, operating system version, language, country, and IP address. An IP address indicates roughly where a device is — typically the city — and is used to serve ads relevant to that region. We do not collect GPS or precise location.

Game progress. Your score and level are stored on your device. When you open the leaderboard, your score is sent to Unity Gaming Services so it can be ranked against other players.

An anonymous player ID. Unity Gaming Services generates a random ID for your device so the leaderboard can tell players apart between sessions. It is not linked to you, and we cannot use it to identify you.

What is not collected

No name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, files, microphone or camera access, precise location, or payment information. The game contains no purchases.

Who it is shared with

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) serves the ads and mediates the ad networks that fill them — currently Unity Ads. They receive the advertising ID and device information described above. See the ironSource privacy policy and the Unity player privacy policy.
  • Unity Gaming Services hosts the leaderboard and receives the anonymous player ID and score. See the Unity privacy policy.

Nothing is sold, and no data is shared with anyone else.

Your choices

Reset or delete the advertising ID. On Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can reset the ID or delete it entirely, which stops advertisers from building a profile across apps. Ads will still appear, but they will not be personalised.

Consent in the EEA and UK. Where the GDPR applies, the game asks for your consent before any personalised advertising runs, and you can decline. If you decline, the game still works and still shows ads — they are simply not personalised.

Delete your data. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on the device. To have a leaderboard entry removed, write to the address below.

Children

Merge Depot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the current version is always at this address.

Contact

Write to the developer contact address shown on the game's Google Play listing.
